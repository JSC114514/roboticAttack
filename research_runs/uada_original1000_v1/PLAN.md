# Original Exploring→OFT UADA 500/750/1000 baseline

2026-09-29. This is the user's requested **original-style UADA baseline**, not the `uada` arm in `wrist_dino_scratch_v2`. V2's `uada` shares a 250-outer prefix, uses a fixed training placement and TV hinge, and is the internal matched control. Here we use `white_patch/UADA_OFT_DINO.py::exploring_oft_uada` unchanged with the original Exploring-derived patch initialization, random transform, AdamW, clamp, opposite-boundary action loss, and outer cosine schedule. The upstream `white_patch/UADA.py` remains read-only (SHA256 `808b08ec686dd5bb85d006963cf79a67c0a8e778b9ba7464c30236cc345fca2e`). There is no DINO auxiliary loss, TV term, warm start, shared V2 prefix or V2 experience bank. The OFT adaptation uses Object-OFT continuous eight-step action prediction, dimension 0 as in the earlier transfer baseline, Front+Wrist input with the patch only in Wrist. Training data are the frozen 300-record Object bank; seed 42 and data seed 20260920.

One fresh run trains 1000 outer × 50 inner = 50,000 optimizer updates and saves the same patch's 500/750/1000 checkpoints. Thus the checkpoints are repeated measures from **one training seed**, not three independent replicates. The new 500 checkpoint has a 1000-outer cosine schedule and must **not** be identified with the deleted historical UADA500 file, which used a 500-outer schedule. Nor is this old frozen UADA2000, whose binary was lost. No old experiment is rerun.

After training, an automatic CPU artifact/lineage check gates evaluation. This is not a human visual judgement: inspect the three PNGs before claiming they look acceptable. Evaluation runs 3 conditions × 4 suites × 10 tasks × 10 frozen states = 1200 fresh attack episodes, paired on task/state/rollout seed and frozen clean eligibility (expected eligible denominators 99/90/98/95). Eight RTX3090 jobs at a time; `libero_10` is measured but remains secondary. Statistics give per-suite CASR and paired checkpoint contrasts; if v2's complete aggregate is present, also give same-budget v2-vs-original-baseline contrasts. Such cross-lineage contrasts are operational performance comparisons, **not** causal DINO ablations, because the training bank/placement/TV/prefix differ. For DINO attribution use v2's matched internal UADA control.
